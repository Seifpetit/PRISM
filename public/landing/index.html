How it works

Four steps. One board. Zero guesswork.

Every step is built from the one before it and stays linked to it. Scroll through one product going the whole way.

01Plan · Strategy

Map who it’s for before you spend a cent.

Drop in a product link, photos, your notes. Prism lays out the audiences, the angle that reaches each one, and the offer that closes. One board, zero sticky notes.

02Make · Creatives

Every angle becomes an ad.

UGC scripts, statics, carousels, written for the person they’re meant for, not for “everyone 18–65.”

03Ship · Pipeline

Brief to live, minus the group chat.

Production runs on the same board, so nothing drifts from the plan and nobody asks “wait, which version?”

04Learn · Performance

Every result traces back.

When an ad wins, you see which audience and angle made it win. Your next move stops being a guess.

Learn from every dollar

Every dollar comes back with notes.

Most dashboards tell you what happened. Prism tells you why, and what to try next. Even the AI’s own cost is itemized, to the fraction of a cent.
